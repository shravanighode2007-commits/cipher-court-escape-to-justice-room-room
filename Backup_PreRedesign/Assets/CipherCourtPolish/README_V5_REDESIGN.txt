CIPHER COURT — FULL REFERENCE-STYLE REDESIGN V5

This version focuses on scene design only and keeps existing puzzle objects, colliders, interactions and gameplay scripts in place.

Visual changes:
- Every gameplay level receives a denser furniture layout using the included VNB Office Set 2 assets.
- Level 1: executive investigation office with desk, chair, sofa, coffee table, shelving, drawers, lamp, wall art and books.
- Level 2: digital forensics room with computer desk, operator chair, PC accessories, shelving, drawers, server racks and warmer lighting.
- Level 3: evidence lab with central evidence table, matching shelves, chairs, evidence cabinet and framed wall art.
- Level 4: secure archive with open shelving (not a giant solid wall), catalog desk, archive chair, ladder, file pile and lamp.
- Level 5: rebuilt as a complete case-analysis/conference room with table, several chairs, shelving, wall display, notes, cabinets and lamps.
- Level 6: professional courtroom with judge dais/bench, witness and clerk areas, counsel tables, spectator benches, judge chair, books, lamps and central wall art.
- Clue primitive box renderers are hidden where possible; clues are represented visually with notepads, sticky notes, document stacks, folders and evidence seals while keeping their interaction objects/colliders intact.
- Door openings receive thick jambs, outer casing and a threshold to hide visible spacing around the moving door.
- Behind every exit is a fully finished passage plus a small next-area vignette (desk/computer, archive shelf/files, or legal decor), so opening a door no longer reveals an empty black space.
- Main Menu and Game Complete screens use new cinematic legal background images included under CipherCourtPolish/Backgrounds.
- Lighting is brighter and warmer to match the supplied reference style while retaining contrast.

The V5 polish applies automatically once when Unity imports the project. It can also be re-run from:
Cipher Court > Apply Final Visual Polish
